Local-First & Zero-Knowledge

Sada Hisaab Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 7, 2026

Sada Hisaab is a privacy-first, local-first financial ledger, cash management, debt tracking, and khums accounting tool developed by the Sada Suite team. For privacy inquiries or support, contact us at fahmatics@gmail.com.

1. Data Ownership & Local Storage

Sada Hisaab operates on a local-first architecture:

  • Ledger Entries & Accounts: All your financial accounts, currency transactions, loans, borrowing records, and khums calculations are stored directly on your physical device using IndexedDB (Dexie).
  • No Cloud Banking Aggregators: We do not connect to external banking APIs (such as Plaid or Yodlee) or collect your real bank login credentials.
  • Zero Third-Party Trackers: The application does not bundle advertising trackers, commercial analytics, or third-party behavioral profiling SDKs.

2. Optional Cross-App Synchronization (Sada Suite)

If you choose to synchronize notes and expenses with companion apps (SadaLink or SadaFlow):

  • Zero-Knowledge Encryption: Cross-app packets are encrypted on your device using AES-256-GCM with keys derived from your PIN and phone number via PBKDF2/HKDF before leaving your device.
  • Relay Infrastructure: The relay server only routes client-encrypted packets and cannot read your financial transactions or plaintext account balances.

3. Permissions

  • Notifications: Used solely to alert you to scheduled khums dates or debt repayment milestones that you configure locally.
  • Camera / File Access: Used only when you choose to scan a physical receipt or upload an invoice attachment to a transaction.

4. Data Deletion & Export

Because your data is stored locally on your device:

  • You can export your complete ledger history at any time as JSON, Excel (XLSX), or PDF via Settings > Export Data.
  • Clearing the app storage or browser cache immediately and permanently purges all local financial records from your device.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy or your data, please contact developer support at fahmatics@gmail.com.
